The Data Platform

A Complete Clinician Picture, Built From Public Data

BioTechForce assembles the profile of every clinician in your therapeutic area from public sources, keeps it current on a schedule, and delivers it as native Salesforce records your team already knows how to use.

Discover

Choose a specialty and a geography, preview the size of the sweep, and import clinicians and the institutions they practice at. Health systems are resolved to one account per institution, not one per identifier, so reps see a clean hierarchy instead of duplicates.

  • Specialty and State Targeting
  • Organization Discovery
  • Health System Hierarchies
  • Affiliation Matching
  • Plan Before You Run

Enrich

Every clinician is enriched with their publications, clinical trials, industry payments, state licenses, and hospital affiliations. Each source is a separate sync you can run, schedule, or skip.

  • Publications and Authorship
  • Clinical Trials and PI Roles
  • Industry Payments
  • State Licensure
  • Hospital Affiliations

Classify

Publications and trials are tagged by indication, mechanism of action, modality, study phase, and patient population against a controlled vocabulary that ships with the package and can be extended per customer without code.

  • Indication and Therapeutic Area
  • Mechanism and Modality
  • Study Phase
  • Versioned Vocabulary
  • Confidence on Every Tag

Keep Current

Weekly and monthly pipelines run every stage in dependency order, each starting when the one before it finishes, so scores are never computed on stale data. Territories are assigned from postal codes automatically.

  • Scheduled Pipelines
  • Dependency-Ordered Stages
  • Job Monitoring
  • Zip-to-Territory Assignment
  • Data Change Requests

Intelligence

KOL Scoring and Rising Star Detection

Two scoring engines read the enriched data. One tells you who leads the field today. The other tells you who will lead it next.

KOL Scoring

Every clinician receives a KOL score across research, clinical, and industry dimensions, and is placed in a development phase that advances as evidence accumulates. Scores are explainable: each one shows the figures that produced it.

  • Per-area scores and an overall phase on every clinician record
  • Dashboards for KOL leaders, KOL development, and field activity
  • Rescored automatically at the end of every pipeline run

Rising Stars

The Rising Star engine looks for momentum rather than reputation: clinicians whose publication velocity, trial trajectory, topical alignment, competitive whitespace, and network position suggest they are about to matter. The engine is backtested against multi-year historical data to confirm the signals predict forward activity.

  • Story candidates: each rising star comes with a headline and the evidence behind it
  • Today's Story on the home page: the one clinician most worth a conversation today
  • Five signals, each visible and weighted, so a rep can see why a name is on the list

Agentforce and Generative AI

AI That Works From Your Data, Not Around It

Every AI feature is grounded in the records in your org and sits on top of a deterministic path that works without it. Nothing in BioTechForce requires generative AI to function, and nothing it generates is unsourced.

Agentforce Field Assistant

An Agentforce employee agent that lets reps and MSLs log a call in plain language, list the story candidates at an account, and ask why a clinician was scored the way they were. Every call log is drafted for review before anything is saved.

  • Conversational Call Logging
  • Draft, Review, Then Save
  • Story Candidates by Account
  • Explain a Score

Pre-Call HCP Brief

One click on a clinician record produces a briefing built from their recent publications, trials, affiliations, and interaction history. Grounded in the record, so it never invents a paper or a role.

  • Recent Research Summary
  • Trial Involvement
  • Interaction History
  • Suggested Talking Points

Industry Payments Narrative

Turns a clinician's public industry payment history into a short, sourced narrative: which companies, what kinds of engagement, and how it has changed over time. The underlying totals and public profile link are always present, AI or not.

  • Payment History Summary
  • Company Relationships
  • Trend Over Time
  • Link to Public Record

Classification That Knows When to Abstain

The record classifier tags publications and trials against your vocabulary and is gated to abstain rather than guess. Off-topic records come back unclear, out-of-vocabulary values never reach a field, and every tag carries a confidence.

  • Controlled Vocabulary Only
  • Abstains on Off-Topic Records
  • Confidence on Every Tag
  • Tested Through the Packaged Install
